# Writing PRs

`gh pr create --body` bypasses `.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md` (GitHub only auto-fills it in the web form), so uniformity comes from writing every PR body to the same shape.

## Body structure

Use these headers, in this order:

```markdown
## Summary

One or two lines: what the PR changes.

## Context

- Why it is needed: the problem or motivation.
- Linked issues, e.g. Closes #123.

## Testing

- Commands run.
- What you observed, and anything not covered.

## Risk & rollback

Blast radius and how to revert. Note any breaking change or migration.
```

## Rules

- **Context and Testing are bullet lists.** Summary and Risk & rollback are short prose.
- **No em dashes (—).** Use commas, colons, or periods. Applies to PR bodies, the template, and commit messages.
- **Human voice.** Write like you are briefing a colleague, not filling a form.
- **No footer.** Never add "Generated with Claude Code" or any "generated by" line.
- **Drop empty sections** rather than leaving a heading with nothing under it.

## Commits

Conventional Commits, one-line subject, no em dashes. No `Co-Authored-By` trailer. Keep PRs single-concern and small (around ten files or fewer).
